# High-Fidelity Page Annotations

## Homepage

The selected reference remains the visual source of truth. Preserve the 50/50 hero,
large problem-led statement, edge-diffused photography, warm paper continuation,
oversized tension statement, horizontal Roadmap progression, dark proof panel, and
paired About/Insights close.

Intentional phase-four changes:

- Shift the dark field from green-black to deep Atlantic blue.
- Brighten the paper field without turning it clinical white.
- Keep clay only for action, focus, nodes, and micro-rules.
- Keep the hero solution framing ahead of process explanation.

## Roadmap

Use the same deep-blue hero language with a photography or supplied geometry panel.
The body is a calm alternating editorial grid with clear links to How It Works,
Results, and FAQ. Process visualization may be denser than normal copy but must
remain understandable in source order on mobile.

## About

The portrait is the strongest asset and should share the hero with the introduction.
Biography and verified authority use ruled editorial sections, not credential cards.
The Roadmap origin and authored ideas create the bridge back to the commercial path.

## Article

The title is the dominant visual element on paper. Breadcrumbs, topic, author, and
date remain quiet. Body measure is 42–46rem. Related content uses ruled teasers after
the complete article; the commercial Roadmap module is visually distinct but not
interruptive.

## Insights and topics

Formats are prominent navigation destinations. Topics use an ordered ruled grid.
Listing pages prioritize one featured/current item and then a restrained chronological
collection. Avoid card dashboards and filter chrome unless content volume proves the
need.

## Forms

Expectation copy and the form share a two-column editorial frame. Mobile reads the
expectation first. The design includes disabled, focused, invalid, submitting,
failure, and success states, but production submission remains withheld until the
integration gate is cleared.

## Mobile

Preserve hierarchy rather than desktop geometry. Copy precedes imagery, process steps
become vertical in the production treatment, paired columns stack, and buttons become
full-width only below 448px. Photography must keep Jack's face unobscured at every
crop.
