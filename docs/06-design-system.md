# Design System Direction

## 1. Brand objective

The site should communicate:

- Intelligence
- Depth
- Warmth
- Confidence
- Experience
- Premium positioning
- Personal character
- Calm

Avoid:

- Corporate template aesthetics
- Tech-startup aesthetics
- Generic motivational-coach aesthetics
- Excessive visual effects
- Overly luxurious "black and gold" clichés

## 2. Creative direction

Reference mood:

**Modern editorial publication + premium personal brand + subtle intellectual geometry**

The content should feel like it has room to breathe.

## 3. Visual language

Use:

- Large editorial type
- Generous whitespace
- Strong photography
- Thoughtful asymmetry
- Fine rules/dividers
- Pull quotes
- Occasional oversized words/numbers
- Subtle circles/arcs/intersections/path motifs
- Restrained motion

Avoid:

- Card grid on every section
- Icon + title + description repeated everywhere
- Giant gradients
- Glassmorphism
- Constant rounded rectangles
- Decorative illustrations unrelated to Jack
- Stock photography of executives

## 4. Typography

Use two families maximum in normal UI:

### Display

Editorial, distinctive, confident.

Use for:
- H1
- Major section headlines
- Pull quotes

### Text/UI

Highly readable.

Use for:
- Body
- Navigation
- Metadata
- Buttons
- Forms

Suggested scale should include:

- Hero display
- H1
- H2
- H3
- Body large
- Body
- Small
- Metadata

Exact fonts must be approved before final implementation.

## 5. Color

Use:

- Warm or neutral page background
- High-contrast text
- One restrained brand accent
- Optional secondary neutral family

Accent is for:

- CTA
- Links
- Small rules
- Highlights
- Branded geometry

Do not use the accent on every surface.

## 6. Spacing

Favor fewer, larger layout intervals over densely packed sections.

Desktop:
- Large vertical section separation.
- Constrained reading width.
- Wider layouts for hero/media sections.

Mobile:
- Preserve breathing room.
- Reduce decorative geometry before reducing readability.

## 7. Photography

Prioritize original Jack photography.

Required image categories:

- Hero portrait/environmental shot
- Conversational/candid
- Speaking/coaching
- Editorial close-up
- Podcast
- Book
- Client/video thumbnails

Photography should feel human and premium rather than staged corporate stock.

## 8. Motion

Allowed:

- Small reveal/fade/slide
- Subtle geometry movement
- Button/underline interactions
- Video hover behavior
- Navigation transitions

Rules:

- Motion must not delay content.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Avoid scroll-jacking.
- Avoid looping decorative animation near reading content.

## 9. Component style

Buttons:
- Primary
- Secondary
- Text link

Cards:
- Use only when the information genuinely benefits from enclosure.

Quotes:
- Large editorial treatment.

Testimonials:
- Video-first where strong footage exists.

Forms:
- Large, calm fields.
- Clear labels.
- Minimal visual noise.

## 10. Homepage visual rhythm

Recommended:

1. High-impact editorial hero
2. Quiet narrative
3. Roadmap mechanism
4. Outcome storytelling
5. Strong video/social proof
6. Editorial Jack biography
7. Content/library area
8. Podcast
9. High-impact final CTA

Alternate light/dark sections only if the composition benefits from it; do not alternate mechanically.
