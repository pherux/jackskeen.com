project owner's 2026-08-31 approval of the content-strategy direction is the
decision baseline. Missing factual content, proof, legal copy, and integration

## Decisions carried forward

- The Roadmap is the primary commercial narrative and conversion path.
- `Start Your Roadmap` is the single global primary CTA and resolves to `/start`.
- Insights support discovery and authority without becoming a parallel conversion
  funnel.
- Existing article URLs remain at their audited root-level paths where practical.
- The eight approved topics organize discovery without recreating WordPress
  category or tag archives.
- Pages remain noindex while their unique approved copy or migrated body is
  missing.
