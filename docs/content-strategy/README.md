## Working rule

“Source-backed” means the statement appears on the current public site. It does not
mean the statement is approved, current, substantiated, or cleared for republication.
Anything involving credentials, client identity, company identity, quantified impact,
pricing, availability, testimonials, or legal commitments requires explicit approval.
