# Positioning and Messaging Recommendation

## Decision status

This document recommends a direction. It does not record final approval.

## Recommended strategic position

Jack Skeen should be positioned as the experienced guide for accomplished people who
have achieved conventional success but want a clearer understanding of their unique
strengths, purpose, and next chapter.

The Roadmap should be positioned as the flagship discovery process—not a generic
coaching package. Its source-backed mechanism is a combination of assessments,
perspectives from people who know the participant well, conversations with Jack,
pattern analysis, and a personalized written Roadmap.

## Working positioning statement

> Jack helps accomplished leaders understand their unique strengths, purpose, and
> next chapter.

Status: already used in the selected homepage direction; final editorial approval is
still required.

## Core problem and solution

### Problem

External success does not automatically create fulfillment, direction, or confidence
about what comes next. The audience may have achievement, responsibility, and options
while lacking a clear view of the patterns, strengths, motivations, and trade-offs
shaping the next chapter.

### Solution

The Roadmap gives the participant structured outside perspective and a synthesis of
what the evidence reveals. The deliverable is described on the current site as a
personalized report intended to bring understanding, clarity, and direction.

### Why Jack

The defensible narrative should be based on verified experience, training, pattern
recognition, personal attention, and the development of The Roadmap. The current site
contains broad authority claims but does not provide enough accessible evidence to
publish a definitive credentials narrative. See the approval register.

## Primary audiences

1. Accomplished executives and founders who are successful but not fully satisfied.
2. High-performing professionals approaching a transition, sale, retirement, career
   change, or new chapter.
3. Referred prospects verifying Jack’s identity, experience, philosophy, and offer.
4. Organizations considering executive coaching, team development, leadership
   development, or culture work.
5. Readers and listeners discovering Jack through articles, video, podcast, or books.

## Message hierarchy

1. **Tension:** success and fulfillment are not the same thing.
2. **Recognition:** it is difficult to see one’s own patterns and contribution clearly.
3. **Mechanism:** assessments, stakeholder perspective, direct conversations, and
   expert synthesis create a fuller picture.
4. **Deliverable:** a personalized Roadmap that captures the findings and priorities.
5. **Evidence:** approved client stories, verified credentials, and visible examples.
6. **Action:** start a conversation to determine fit.

## Offer hierarchy

Recommended order from the product requirements:

1. The Roadmap — flagship one-to-one discovery process.
2. Roadmap Essentials — a more focused version; current public details require review.
3. Roadmap Discovery Group — no usable source material found; definition required.
4. Executive coaching — individual leadership work.
5. Corporate advisory — team, leadership-development, and culture work; scope needs
   consolidation and naming approval.

Do not imply that the offers are interchangeable. The Work With Jack page should help
visitors self-select by need, format, and depth without creating an unverified pricing
comparison.

## Language to prefer

- Clear, direct statements about the audience’s tension.
- Concrete descriptions of what happens in the process.
- “Outside perspective,” “patterns,” “unique strengths,” “purpose,” and “next chapter.”
- Jack’s actual point of view, examples, and named frameworks.
- Client language only when the source and publication permission are confirmed.

## Language to avoid or verify

- “Life-changing,” “transformative,” “extraordinary,” “renowned,” “top,” or “proven”
  unless the context and substantiation are approved.
- Manufactured scarcity or availability claims.
- ROI, business-result, productivity, satisfaction, or retention claims without proof.
- “Superpower” as the dominant term; the current site uses it, but “unique genius” or
  “unique contribution” is more consistent with the planned information architecture.
- Unverified company logos, client roles, credentials, degrees, or professional titles.
- Prices, payment terms, discounts, and geographic availability until reconfirmed.

## CTA recommendation

Primary label: **Start Your Roadmap**.

Recommended behavior: lead to `/start`, where the visitor can understand fit and make
an inquiry. Whether that route ends in a native form, a scheduler, or a combined flow
is unresolved. Do not use “apply” unless qualification and acceptance are real parts of
the process.

Secondary labels may use the approved requirements language: Meet Jack, Explore
Insights, Watch Client Stories, Listen to Inside the Circle, and Start a Conversation.
