# Three-Month Editorial Calendar Framework

## Status

This is a research and interview plan, not publish-ready content. Titles are working
questions drawn from `docs/05-seo-ai-search.md`. Every piece requires Jack’s substantive
answer, editorial review, and source verification before publication.

## Publishing cadence

- One substantive article or edited transcript per week.
- One supporting podcast/video item when source material exists.
- One newsletter note that points to the primary piece rather than duplicating it.
- No quota-driven filler and no thin programmatic FAQ pages.

## Editorial requirements for every piece

1. Give a direct answer early.
2. Preserve Jack’s actual language and point of view.
3. Add a specific example only when approved.
4. State nuance and limitations.
5. Link to the primary topic and two to five relevant pieces.
6. Use one contextual commercial CTA where appropriate.
7. Record author, original date, editor, sources, and approval status.
8. Create transcript, summary, and clips from the same source only after rights review.

## Measurement

Track indexation, impressions, clicks, qualified CTA visits, newsletter signups, and
assisted inquiries. Do not evaluate the plan only by total page views.
